# Service category photographs

These bundled stock photographs illustrate service categories. They are not
provider portraits, premises, or examples of a provider's work. They are used by
Discover's illustrated card variant; provider records and Firestore are unchanged.

All 13 photographs were selected from free Unsplash photo pages under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), checked on 2026-11-13.
Photographer credits, original photo pages, and download URLs are recorded in
[sources.json](./sources.json). No Unsplash+ images are included.

JPEGs are 640 × 640, cropped and compressed by the source CDN for small mobile
thumbnails, and bundled locally for offline display. The tanker photo is a
generic illustration of tank vehicles, not an actual local sewage truck.
